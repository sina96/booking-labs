---
name: recap
description: Weekly spaced-retrieval quiz on previously completed labs, to build long-term retention.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

1. Read `PROGRESS.md`, the recap log and `learning-records/`. Pick 6 questions: weight older and weaker topics more, and mix topics (interleaving).
2. Questions must be **recall**, not recognition: "explain", "what happens when", "draw the sequence", "what would you check first". At most 1 multiple-choice question.
3. Ask them one at a time. Sina answers from memory, without looking at the notes. Give feedback after each one: correct / partially / wrong + the missing piece.
4. Append a line to the recap log in `PROGRESS.md` (date, topics, score, what to revisit).
